Gemhold – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Gemhold is a free game made by one hobby developer. It has no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no tracking. This page explains the little data the game uses.

Without an account

If you never create an account, nothing leaves your device. Your progress is saved only on your phone or computer.

With an account (optional)

In the Hall of Fame you can create an account to appear on the leaderboard, keep your achievements and play online races. Then the game stores:

  • Your email address and password, used only to sign you in and to reset a forgotten password. They are handled by Google Firebase Authentication; the password is never visible to the developer.
  • Your player name, scores, stars, achievements and race wins/losses. These are shown publicly on the in-game leaderboard. Your email is never shown.
  • Race progress (wave, gems, kills, score) while an online race is running, visible to your rival.

This data is stored with Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), operated by Google on the developer's behalf. It is not sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising. Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Deleting your account

You can delete your account and all its online data at any time:

  • in the game: Hall of Fame → Delete account, or
  • on the web: fildim.github.io/gemhold/delete-account.html.

Deletion is immediate and permanent. Progress saved on your own device is not affected.

Children

Gemhold is not directed at children under 13, and accounts are not intended for them.
