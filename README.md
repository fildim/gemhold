# Gemhold

**You are the dark lord, and the heroes want your gems.**

Deep in your cave lie five gems. Self-righteous heroes march down the roads in waves to steal them: peasants, scouts, armoured knights, healers and worse. Raise towers on moss, frost and ash, rain meteors on the crowds, cut down the woods to make room, and let not a single gem leave your lair.

Eight lairs, each with its own battle music, a night mission to unlock in every one, and a tree of Dark Arts to grow stronger with the stars you earn.

## How to start

- **Browser, iPhone or iPad:** tap **Play now**. Nothing to install. On an iPhone or iPad, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**, and it opens full screen like an app.
- **Windows:** download and double-click `Gemhold.exe`. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **macOS:** download and unzip, then right-click **Gemhold** and choose **Open**, then **Open** again (only the first time).
- **Linux:** download, unzip and run `Gemhold.x86_64`.
- **Android:** download `Gemhold.apk` on your phone and open it. Allow installing from your browser when asked.

Your progress saves by itself. The first lair teaches you as you play, and the **Guide** on the map explains everything else.
